## Welcome to the studio

Since 2009, the Saint-Jean Dance School has welcomed dancers from the Québec City area in a warm but demanding environment. Our teaching focuses on technique, musicality and the sheer pleasure of moving.

## One session, many paths

Each session runs for fifteen weeks and ends with a show at Théâtre Petit Champlain. Trial classes are free during the first two weeks of the fall session.

## Registration

Registration for the fall session opens on August 15. Drop by our rue Saint-Jean studio or write to us from the contact page.
